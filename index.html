How to use your garden

Plant: choose Flowers, pick a specimen, then tap or drag across the meadow. Move: tap a flower or its circle, then drag to reposition it. Use Size and Tilt to adjust it. Undo brings back your last change.

Keyboard: focus the meadow and use the arrows to move the planting marker; Space or Enter plants. P selects Plant, E selects Move. In Move mode, Tab reaches each flower; Enter selects it, arrows move it, and Delete removes it. Ctrl/Command + Z undoes a change. Escape closes this menu or finishes editing.
