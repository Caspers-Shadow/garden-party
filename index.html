A few notes on tending your garden

Click or drag on the meadow to plant. Choose Arrange, then drag a flower to move it. Use the size and tilt controls to shape your arrangement.

Keyboard: focus the meadow and use the arrow keys to move the planting marker; Space or Enter plants a flower. P selects Plant, E selects Arrange. In Arrange, Tab reaches each specimen; Enter selects it, arrow keys move it, and Delete removes it. Ctrl/Command + Z undoes your last change.
